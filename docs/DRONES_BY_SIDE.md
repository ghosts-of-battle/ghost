# Ghost drones by side

> **Archive.** The faction addons this describes are no longer in `addons/` --
> they were moved to `bad/`. Kept as a record of what they fielded.

Every drone class the Ghost factions field, grouped by side. Taken from the
faction configs in `bad/` and `old/` -- the faction addons are no longer in
`addons/`, so this is a record of what they carried, not what the mod ships.

Split three ways because the configs mix them: **drone** is the flying/driving
vehicle, **bag** is its backpack form, **operator** is the infantryman who
carries the terminal (a soldier, not a drone). UAV terminals are left out.
